NATIONAL RAGGING PREVENTION PROGRAMME Ragging is a CRIME Ragging can lead to Suspension, Rustication or Police arrest In pursuance to the UGC guidelines, CHARUSAT has constituted an Anti-Ragging Committee. The Committee is committed to eliminating ragging in all its forms. Mechanism CHARUSAT has zero-tolerance towards ragging in any form. Any individual who is subjected to ragging by any student or any other person can report the incident to the member/Convener/Chairman of Anti-ragging Committee or Head of the Institute/Department. The Anti-ragging Committee will take prompt and appropriate action on receipt of the complaint in line with the guidelines issued by UGC.

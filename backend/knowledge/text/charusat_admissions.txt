Project Based Learning State of the Art Research Facilities Holistic Development Message from Principals Desk It’s my immense pleasure to introduce Bapubhai Desaibhai Patel Institute of Allied and Healthcare Sciences (BDIAS) constituent of Charotar University of Science and technology (CHARUSAT) established in 2016. CHARUSAT has been established by promulgation of Gujarat Private University Act, 2009. It is empowered to confer degrees under Section 22 of UGC Act, 1956. Our university received grade A+ by NAAC and KCG. Our institute is offering four paramedical courses since in the hi-tech world of modern health care, the need for a team approach in curing illness and sustaining life is essential and it takes people with varying skills and expertise to make up the team. I believe advanced complex instrumentation and equipment require technocrats not only to operate but also to care and maintain them as well. Paramedical experts have to possess a strong scientific foundation and be able to perform at a much higher level than the traditionally trained technicians of the past.

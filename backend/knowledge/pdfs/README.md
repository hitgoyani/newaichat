# Sample University Handbook PDF Content

## Note for Testing

Since we cannot create actual PDF files programmatically in a simple way here, this is a text representation of what would be in `university_handbook.pdf`.

In a real implementation, you would:
1. Create a PDF using tools like:
   - Microsoft Word (save as PDF)
   - Google Docs (download as PDF)
   - Online PDF creators
   - Python libraries like ReportLab

2. Content for the PDF:

---

# UNIVERSITY HANDBOOK 2024-25
# STUDENT POLICIES AND GUIDELINES

## PART 1: CODE OF CONDUCT

### Article 1: General Behavior
All students are expected to maintain decorum and discipline on campus premises. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under law. Students must respect faculty, staff, and fellow students at all times.

### Article 2: Academic Integrity
Plagiarism, copying, cheating, or any form of academic dishonesty is a serious offense. Students found guilty will face disciplinary action including suspension or expulsion.

### Article 3: Attendance and Punctuality
Regular attendance is mandatory. Students must arrive on time for all classes, labs, and examinations. Persistent lateness will attract penalties.

## PART 2: FACILITIES

### Library Facilities
- Open 8 AM to 10 PM on weekdays
- Open 9 AM to 5 PM on weekends
- Over 50,000 books and journals
- Digital library access
- Study rooms available for group discussions
- Silent reading zones

### Computer Labs
- 5 computer labs with 300+ workstations
- High-speed internet connectivity
- Access timings: 8 AM - 8 PM
- Software: Latest IDEs, design tools, office suites
- Printing facility available (₹5 per page)

### Sports Facilities
- Cricket ground
- Football field
- Basketball court
- Indoor badminton courts
- Gymnasium (membership ₹1,000 per year)
- Swimming pool (membership ₹2,000 per year)

## PART 3: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES

### Minor Offenses
- Warning letter
- Community service
- Temporary suspension of privileges

### Major Offenses
- Suspension for one semester
- Permanent expulsion
- Legal action (for criminal activities)

### Appeal Process
Students have the right to appeal any disciplinary action within 15 days to the Student Grievance Committee.

## PART 4: HOSTEL RULES

### Check-in/Check-out
- Check-in: After 8 AM
- Check-out: Before 10 PM
- Late entry requires prior permission

### Visitor Policy
- Visitors allowed in common areas only
- Visiting hours: 5 PM - 8 PM
- Overnight guests not permitted

### Maintenance
- Room inspection conducted monthly
- Damage to property will be charged
- Cleanliness must be maintained

## Instructions for Creating the Actual PDF:

1. Copy the content above into Microsoft Word or Google Docs
2. Format it nicely with headings, proper spacing
3. Save/Export as PDF with filename: `university_handbook.pdf`
4. Place the PDF in: `backend/knowledge/pdfs/university_handbook.pdf`

The PDF parser will then automatically extract this text when the server starts.
